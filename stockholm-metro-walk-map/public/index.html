          About this map

          Unofficial accessibility visualisation. Not affiliated with or endorsed by SL (Storstockholms Lokaltrafik), Region Stockholm, Samtrafiken or Trafiklab, or by any company shown.

          What the shaded areas show

          Each shaded area is the land you can reach on foot within 5 or 10 minutes of walking from a Stockholm metro (tunnelbana) station, at about 1.4 m/s (5 km/h). It does not include any time on the train. A pedestrian routing engine calculated the areas on the walkable street and path network (footways, paths, crossings and bridges). They are not circles or straight-line distances, so water, railways and motorways only shorten the reach where the network goes around them.

          The "combined coverage" view is a true geometric union of the selected stations' areas, one for each time threshold. A station served by several selected lines counts once.

          Current data

          Method

          • Network. Stations, lines and the order of stations come from SL's own timetable data (GTFS): every distinct stop sequence of every metro trip, in both directions. Two stations are connected only when a train calls at them one after the other. Coordinates are never used to connect stations.
          • Stations. One physical station can have several operator records (platforms, separate parent records). They are merged only on an ID link, a documented alias or an equal name, and only within 500 m.
          • Lines. Drawn in SL's line colours, as straight segments between adjacent stations, not along the exact track. Where two lines share track (red and green between Slussen and T-Centralen), they are drawn side by side.
          • Properties. A property counts as within N minutes when its point lies inside the N-minute walking area of any selected station. Shares are cumulative (≤ 10 min includes ≤ 5 min).

          Limitations

          • Each walk starts from one coordinate per station, the operator's station centroid. Real entrances can be a few hundred metres away, so the real walk from a given entrance can be a minute or more shorter or longer.
          • Results depend on OpenStreetMap data and the routing engine's pedestrian profile. Mapping gaps, steps and lifts, gates and opening hours may be missing or simplified. Construction closures and temporary diversions are not represented.
          • Property positions were geocoded from street addresses. Some are only matched to the street or the district, not the building. Each property's popup shows its match level. Properties that could not be located, and the two Borås properties (outside the network), are not counted in the shares.
          • Floor area is the source's total lettable area. Properties with 0 or no floor area in the source are treated as unknown and left out of floor-area shares (not counted as zero).

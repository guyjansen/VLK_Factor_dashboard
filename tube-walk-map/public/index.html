          About this map

          Unofficial accessibility visualisation. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Transport for London.

          What the shaded areas show

          Each shaded area is the land you can reach on foot within 5 or 10 minutes of walking from a London Underground or Elizabeth line station. It does not include any time on the train. The areas were calculated by a pedestrian routing engine on the walkable street and path network: footways, paths, crossings and bridges. They are not circles or straight-line distances. Rivers, railways, motorways, parks and private land only make a difference where the routing data includes them.

          The "combined coverage" view is a true geometric union of the selected stations' areas, calculated separately for each time threshold. A station served by several selected lines counts once.

          Current data

          Scope

          The map covers 12 lines: the 11 London Underground lines and the Elizabeth line, including its stations as far out as Reading and Shenfield. Stations where Underground and Elizabeth line platforms form one station, such as Liverpool Street, Paddington and Whitechapel, appear once with all their lines. London Overground, DLR, trams and National Rail are not shown.

          Limitations

          • Each walk starts from one coordinate per station, usually the station centroid supplied in the source data. Real entrances can be up to a few hundred metres away, especially at large interchanges.
          • The walking times start at that one coordinate, so the real walk from a given entrance can be shorter or longer by a minute or more at large stations.
          • Results depend on OpenStreetMap data and the routing provider's pedestrian profile (default speed about 1.4 m/s, or 5 km/h). Mapping gaps, steps and lifts, gates, and opening hours may be missing or simplified.
          • Construction closures, temporary diversions and station access restrictions are not represented.
          • Line routes are drawn as straight segments between adjacent stations in TfL route order. They do not follow the exact track alignment.
